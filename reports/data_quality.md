## Additional findings not stated in the plan

1. **The fertiliser table is not complete across districts.** Only 395 of 646 (mapped crop x district) cells carry a published recipe — 61.1%. Linseed appears in 3 districts of 34, sesame in 9. The plan does not mention this. Because doses genuinely vary by district (1745 of 3144 crop-context keys have more than one distinct quantity across districts), borrowing a neighbour's recipe silently would be wrong; the engine serves a state-wide median instead, labelled as an estimate and carrying its own observed spread.

2. **Four talukas have no recorded soil texture** (Tumsar, Ausa, Latur, Parseoni — all Alluvial). Imputed from the modal texture of their own soil type and flagged with `texture_imputed`. (4 remain missing after imputation.)

3. **The plan's §6.6 crop spellings do not match the table.** It writes `Pearl millet`, `Pigeon pea`, `Finger millet`, `Tetraploid cotton`; the table uses title case. The ontology resolves case-insensitively and is unit-tested against the real 78-crop list.

4. **The plan's §4.2 texture vocabulary does not match the data.** It assumes a Sandy -> Clayey scale; the data carries only Clayey, Loamy and two `-skeletal` variants. Skeletal soils hold >35% coarse fragments, so their fine-earth AWC is discounted ~40%.

5. **Rabi cannot be scored on in-season rainfall.** Rabi rain averages ~60 mm state-wide. Maharashtra's Rabi crops run on monsoon moisture stored in the profile, so the suitability gate adds the root-zone store to the season's rain. Without this the gate vetoes every Rabi crop in the state.
